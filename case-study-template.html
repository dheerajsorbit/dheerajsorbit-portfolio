01. The Problem

Cognitive Overload in Mission-Critical Support

Support agents handling complex enterprise tickets frequently review conversation threads containing dozens of back-and-forth emails, chat transcripts, internal notes, and technical logs. Our user research revealed agents spent up to 4 minutes per ticket simply synthesizing what had already happened before typing a single word.

“I have to scroll through 15 pages of chat history just to understand what the customer already tried. By the time I start writing, our SLA is already yellow.” — Tier-2 Enterprise Support Specialist
02. Design Principles

Human-Centered AI Guardrails

Enterprise agents were wary of automated bot answers that risked hallucinating incorrect policy or technical guidance. We established three core principles:

1. Ambient Presence
The copilot suggests insights silently in a peripheral drawer without hijacking active keyboard focus.
2. Transparent Confidence
Every AI summary and recommendation cites exact source messages so agents verify facts in 1 click.
3. Agent in Control
AI never sends autonomous replies to customers. Agents can accept, adjust tone, or discard with zero friction.
